          Each animal here is an individual with a tiny brain (a neural network) that it inherits, with mutations, from its parents. Those that find food, catch prey, escape and find mates leave more young, so behaviour evolves as you watch.               An evolution sandbox

              StrangeLife

              A newborn planet: scalding seas under a thick CO₂ sky, battered by comets, with no life at all. Make it habitable, or wait for it to cool on its own, and watch life begin. Then follow it as it branches into bacteria, algae, fungi, plants and animals.

              • Look around. Drag to spin the planet. Scroll or pinch to zoom in. Icons show what lives in each region.
              • Kindle life. Life needs liquid water, warm shallow seas and organic molecules. Volcanic vents, lightning and comets make them. The Planet tab tracks your progress.
              • Watch it evolve. Every species has a genome that builds its body: cells, symmetry, segments, legs, shells, bones, wings, a brain. Mutations invent new body plans, and the planet decides which survive, so no two worlds grow the same tree of life.
              • Go in close. Inspect a zone and press Z to watch its animals as individuals whose small brains evolve: foraging, hunting, fleeing, herding.
              • Meddle. Raise continents, warm or freeze the climate, seed organisms, or drop an asteroid and see who survives.

              With a terraforming tool selected, left-drag paints and right-drag spins. Keys: 1–9, 0, -, = tools, Space pause, [ ] speed, T tree of life.
