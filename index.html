        StrangeLife

        A young planet with one form of life: a microscopic swimmer in a warm, shallow sea. Every creature that follows descends from it, each a little different from its parent.

        • Look around. Drag to spin the planet. Scroll or pinch to zoom down to the coastlines.
        • Watch. Speed up time and follow life as it spreads, splits into species, crawls onto land, and starts hunting.
        • Meddle. Raise mountains, warm the poles, flood the lowlands, seed new organisms, or drop a meteor and see who survives.

        With a terraforming tool selected, left-drag paints and right-drag spins. Keys: 1–9 tools, Space pause, [ ] speed.
